Toy and Package

Harvest Mile is a children’s puzzle game centered around exploration, problem-solving, and playful learning. The project includes both the physical toy concept and its packaging, designed to be approachable, colorful, and easy to understand for kids and parents. The packaging prioritizes clear visuals, friendly typography, and storytelling to communicate how the game works at a glance.
